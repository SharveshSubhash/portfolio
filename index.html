// 01 — Profile

I work at the intersection of computer vision, robotics, and deep learning — turning raw sensor streams into perception systems that run on the edge.

Currently a Robotics Autonomy intern at BeeX Autonomous Systems, building self-supervised vision models (LeJEPA, DINOv3) for explainable out-of-distribution anomaly detection in turbid industrial marine environments — deployed across DGX Spark, Jetson Thor and Xavier for AUVs.

Alongside, I'm completing an M.Tech. in AI Systems at the National University of Singapore. My work spans knowledge distillation, self-supervised pretraining for domain-specific imagery, and UAV/underwater systems integrating perception with SLAM — always close to hardware.
